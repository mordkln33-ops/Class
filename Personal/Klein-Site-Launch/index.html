About

A teacher first

My tools come from that classroom thinking: find the moment an idea stops making sense, then let students see and move it until it clicks.

I also work in UX/UI design, so every tool and page starts with one question: what does this student, parent or teacher need right now? Then I make it easy.

How it works

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Why not just use a free tool like Desmos or GeoGebra?

They're excellent, and I recommend them. A custom tool is different: it teaches a topic the way you teach it, in your order and your words, and it carries your name on your own site, where families find you.

Can I get a learning tool without a website?

Yes. Tools work on their own. You get a link you can share with students, post in Google Classroom or add to an existing website.

Do I have to pay a monthly fee?

No. You pay once. Standard hosting is free for most sites, and your domain (about $12–20 a year) is registered in your name. Ongoing support is optional.

How long does it take?

Library tools take a few days. Custom tools take 1–3 weeks. Tutor websites take 1–2 weeks, and tutoring center sites 2–4 weeks.

Who owns what you build?

Custom tools and websites are yours: the code, domain and hosting are all in your name. Library tools are licensed to you for use on your site and with your students.

How do I make changes after launch?

Send changes to me and I'll make them, usually within two business days. The Semester refresh keeps schedules, staff and prices current before each term, and Center sites include the first one.

Do you write the content?

I send a short questionnaire and edit what you write so it reads clearly. If you'd rather not write at all, I can write it from a 30-minute interview.
