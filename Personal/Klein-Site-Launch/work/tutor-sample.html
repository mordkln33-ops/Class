Ruler & Compass Math: a website for an independent math tutor

A three-page site that answers parents' questions before they text, and turns first messages into scheduled sessions.

Client type
Independent tutor
Plan
Tutor + Quick Check
Pages
3
Timeline
2 weeks

The brief

Who
A math tutor teaching Algebra I through Precalculus and SAT math to about 20 high school students a week, in person and online. (Fictional client.)
The situation
Every new student comes from word of mouth. Parents find an old Facebook page, then text the same questions: What do you charge? Which courses? Online or in person? Any openings on Tuesdays? Answering them takes several hours each week.
Goals
Answer common questions before the first message. Make inquiries arrive with everything needed to reply with a time. Look established to parents who don't know the tutor yet. Show up when local parents search for a math tutor.
Constraints
A budget of about $1,200. No time for upkeep. No photos of students, for privacy.

The approach

I started with the five questions parents actually ask and made sure a phone visitor could answer every one within a single scroll of the homepage.

  • Rates shown openly, with a short note on what a session includes. This removes the most common text.
  • An inquiry form that does the sorting: course, grade, goal (catch up, keep up, get ahead or test prep) and preferred times. The tutor can reply with a proposed slot.
  • Trust without student photos: a friendly portrait, a three-sentence teaching approach and parent quotes once collected.
  • A "Text me" button fixed to the bottom of the screen on phones, since that's how parents already reach out.

Site structure

Home
  • Subjects and courses
  • How sessions work
  • Rates
  • Parent questions
About
  • Teaching approach and background
Contact and availability
  • Inquiry form and service area

The design

The interactive tool: "Which course fits?"

A five-question check, with problems drawn from Algebra I through Precalculus, that suggests a starting point. Parents see the tutor's style before the first session, and the result is attached to the inquiry automatically.

Measuring success

What I'd track in the first three months:

  • Inquiries per month, and how many arrive with complete information
  • Hours per week spent answering repeat questions
  • Visitors who arrive from a Google search
