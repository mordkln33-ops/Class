Privacy

Privacy policy

MathVisuals is built to collect as little as possible. The tools work entirely in your browser, there are no ads, no analytics and no tracking cookies, and nothing is ever sold. This page explains the few things that are collected and why.

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Who runs this site

MathVisuals (mathvisuals.online) is run by Mordechai Klein, a math teacher, as a free personal project. I am responsible for the information described on this page.

The short version

  • Using the tools: nothing you type, click or draw in a tool leaves your device.
  • The “Suggest a tool” form: if you send it, I receive what you wrote, including your name and email.
  • Fonts: the site's font is loaded from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address.
  • Hosting: like every website, the web host keeps standard server logs.

The math tools

Every tool runs in your browser using JavaScript. Numbers, equations, graphs and settings you enter are not sent to the server, saved, or seen by anyone else. Refreshing or closing the page clears them.

The “Suggest a tool” form

If you choose to send a suggestion, the form collects:

  • your name and email address
  • your role and grade level, if you choose to give them
  • your tool idea and its description
  • the date and time you sent it

Why: to read your idea and, if I have questions, to reply to you by email. Your email address is never added to a mailing list, used for marketing, or shared.

Where it goes: each suggestion is saved to a file on the web server that is blocked from public access, and a copy is emailed to my Gmail account, so it is also stored by Google.

How long it's kept: only as long as I need it to consider your idea and reply. You can ask me to delete it at any time (see “Your choices” below).

Spam protection: when you press “Send”, the server sets a temporary session cookie so it can limit how often one visitor sends the form. It holds no personal information and is deleted when you close your browser. The form also includes a hidden field and the time the page was loaded; both are only used to block automated spam.

Cookies and local storage

MathVisuals uses no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. It stores only:

  • Your light or dark mode choice, saved in your browser's local storage (as mv-theme) so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device, and you can clear it by clearing your browser's site data.
  • A temporary session cookie, only if you send the suggestion form (described above).

Google Fonts

The site's typeface, Atkinson Hyperlegible, is loaded from Google Fonts. To deliver the font, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive your IP address and basic browser information. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Server logs

The company that hosts this website automatically records basic information about each request, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. This is standard for every website and is used for security and to keep the site running. I don't use these logs to identify visitors.

Children and students

MathVisuals is made for teachers to use at the front of a classroom. Students can use the tools freely, since the tools collect nothing. The suggestion form is meant for adults: if you are under 13, please don't send it, and ask a teacher or parent to send your idea instead. If I learn that a child under 13 has sent personal information, I will delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete anything you sent through the suggestion form. To do that, send a message through the suggestion form using the same email address, or reply to any email I've sent you. I'll respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights under local privacy law, and you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, so what you send through the form is encrypted on its way to the server. Saved suggestions are kept in a folder that is blocked from the web. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but I collect only what's needed and delete it when it's no longer useful.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a new feature is added, the new version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
