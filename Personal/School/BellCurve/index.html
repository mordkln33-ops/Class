Mean

—

The average. Add up every result, then divide by how many results there are. It is the balance point of the dots.

Median

—

The middle result. Line up every result from smallest to largest — half are below this number and half are above.

Standard Deviation

—

How spread out the results are. A small SD means the dots crowd close to the mean; a large SD means they spread out wide.
